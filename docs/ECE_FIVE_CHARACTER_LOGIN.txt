Five-character ECE login investigation — 9 November 2026

Failure
The shared code input accepts five characters and enables submission. codeKind
required exactly eight alphanumeric characters after whitespace/dash removal.
Five-character imports therefore went to OrganizerProvider.signIn and the legacy
claim_organizer_message_code RPC, rather than claimAttendeeAccess/ecg-claim.
Live reproduction with a privately supplied imported token: HTTP 400, SQLSTATE
22023. The raw database message, token and identity were suppressed.

Fix
codeKind accepts five or eight alphanumeric characters, case-insensitively,
preserving existing spaced/dashed ECE codes and prefixed legacy KAMUN routing.
The home screen omits coffee ordering for admins and guards its open handler.
Roles continue to come exclusively from private server verification.
